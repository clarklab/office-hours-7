    Not a Video

    Pressing play does not stream anything. It builds an office, hires six people and runs a directed cutscene at 384×216 — camera cuts, procedural animation, dialogue boxes that type themselves, the lot. Every frame is being rendered right now, on your machine, badly on purpose.

    There are no image files, no model files, no audio files and no fonts. Every texture is drawn onto a canvas at load, every voice is synthesised with WebAudio, and the whole thing is plain ES modules with no build step. If a character sounds like a trombone having a bad quarter, that is a few oscillators and a filter sweep.

    It is a Final Fantasy VII riff about a Series A company whose product is never explained. Nobody is punching down. Everyone is doing their best.
